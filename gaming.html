💡 Gaming Tips & Guides

🔫
Improve Your Aim in FPS Games
Lower your sensitivity slightly and practice in training modes. Track enemies smoothly instead of flick-shooting.
⚙️
Best Sensitivity Settings
Start at medium sensitivity (3-5) and adjust based on your comfort. Lower is better for precise aiming.
⬆️
How to Rank Up Faster
Play with friends, learn the maps, practice regularly, and watch pro player streams for strategies.
🎮
Beginner Gaming Tips
Start with offline games, learn the basics, use earphones for better audio cues, and have fun!
🎧
Audio Advantage
Always use headphones/earphones. Sound cues are crucial for spotting enemies and winning matches.
🛡️
Map Control
Learn the maps, control key positions, use cover effectively, and stay with teammates for safety.
